# PPDAC Analysis: Telecom Customer Churn Prevention
## Step 1: PROBLEM Definition

**Analysis Date:** September 23, 2026  
**Audience:** Board of Directors  
**Dataset:** telecom_churn.csv

---

### Business Objective
Identify the **primary drivers of customer churn** and propose actionable, cost-effective solutions to reduce churn by 5-10% within the coming year.

### Deliverables Expected
1. **Root Cause Analysis**: Data-backed identification of the biggest factors causing churn
2. **Short-term Actions**: Low-cost, quick-win interventions (implementable within 2-3 months)
3. **Long-term Strategy**: One strategic initiative to prevent/reduce churn systematically
4. **Impact Prediction**: Quantified potential churn reduction (target: 5-10%)

### Key Constraints
- **Budget**: Limited - prioritize low-cost, high-impact solutions initially
- **Approval Gate**: Board approval required before implementation across all data teams
- **Validation**: All recommendations must be backed by reliable data evidence

### Success Criteria
✅ Reliable, data-backed identification of churn causes  
✅ Credible prediction of 5-10% churn reduction potential  
✅ Board-ready presentation with actionable recommendations  
✅ Clear prioritization of short-term vs. long-term initiatives  

### Dataset Overview
- **File**: telecom_churn.csv
- **Variables**: 11 features including account tenure, contract renewal, data plan, usage patterns, service calls, and charges
- **Target**: Binary churn indicator (0 = retained, 1 = churned)
- **Size**: ~3,000+ customer records (to be confirmed)

Will follow PPDAC methodology:
1. **Problem** (Current) ✓
2. **Plan** → How to analyze the data
3. **Data** → Explore, clean, and summarize
4. **Analysis** → Statistical and exploratory analysis
5. **Conclusion** → Business recommendations
