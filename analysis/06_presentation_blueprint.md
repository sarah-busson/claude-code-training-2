## SLIDE 1: SCQA OPENING
### "The Churn Opportunity: $343K at Risk, 5-10% Recoverable"

**Narration Structure (Tell the story):**

**S - SITUATION:**
"Our telecom industry faces persistent customer churn. At 14.49%, we're losing nearly 500 customers annually—a natural part of business. But what's NOT natural is accepting it as inevitable."

**C - COMPLICATION:**
"Here's what we discovered: Churn isn't random. We analyzed 3,333 customers and found five statistically clear patterns. A customer without a contract renewal has a 42% churn rate—nearly 4x our baseline. Customers who call support 4 times? They leave 51% of the time. Heavy users without a data plan leave at 27%. It's not a support problem or a pricing problem—it's a pattern problem."

**Q - QUESTION:**
"So we asked ourselves: If churn is predictable, can we prevent it? Which interventions have the highest return? And can we hit our ambitious 5-10% reduction target affordably?"

**A - ANSWER:**
"Yes. Today we're presenting a four-move strategy—implemented over 2-3 months—that achieves 10% churn reduction, saves $200K+ annually, and pays for itself in 12 months. And we found one long-term structural change that makes this sustainable."

**Visual:** 
- Left side: Big "$343K" number (annual revenue at risk)
- Right side: Upward arrow with "5-10% Recoverable" 
- Bottom: Small logos/icons for the 5 churn drivers
